3.2 Characteristics of the included evidence

The preliminary evidence base comprised 319 analytical records: 152 (47.6%) primary empirical records, 141 (44.2%) review-level records, 20 (6.3%) methods, framework or regulatory records, and 6 (1.9%) other non-empirical or unclear records. Publication years ranged from 2016 to 2026 (median 2025); 266 (83.4%) were published from 2023 onwards. The most frequent extracted design categories were mixed-methods (n=50), SR (n=48), scoping (n=40), other (n=36). Review-level records are catalogued separately and do not represent additional independent primary studies.

The most common intervention or object categories were GenAI/LLM chatbot or conversational agent (n=132), Chatbot/conversational agent (architecture mixed or unclear) (n=82), Hybrid chatbot/agent (non-LLM or unclear) (n=43), and the predominant settings were Online (n=121), Mixed (n=87), Not reported (n=66). The extracted age mappings indicated that 81 (25.4%) records included ages 10-19 and 120 (37.6%) included some part of ages 10-24. Explicit GenAI terminology was identified in 147 (46.1%) records, including 70 (46.1%) of primary empirical records. Across all record types, 2 (0.6%) were flagged as covering South Africa, ages 10-19 and explicit GenAI, and 3 (0.9%) met the same criterion using the broader 10-24 age definition. When restricted to primary empirical evidence, the corresponding counts were 0 (0.0%) and 1 (0.7%), respectively. These directness estimates are preliminary: South African review coverage, age mappings and GenAI classifications remain subject to record-level verification and may change in the final report.
